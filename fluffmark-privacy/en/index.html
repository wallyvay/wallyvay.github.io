Privacy Policy

Fluffmark
Last updated: July 30, 2026

Fluffmark imports only the media you explicitly choose through the system photo picker. Image decoding, annotation editing, preview rendering, and export run on this device. Fluffmark does not upload your original media or annotation documents to a server in this version.

Editable projects are stored in the app’s local Application Support directory. You can delete one project or clear all editing history from the Home screen. Exported results are written to Photos only after you choose Save to Photos; the app requests add-only access and does not need full library access.

If you send feedback, your email may include the app version, device type, system version, locale, time zone, and the description you write. Email delivery is handled by your configured mail service.
